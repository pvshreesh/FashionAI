# What to Do After Uploading Images

## 🎉 You've Uploaded Items to Your Wardrobe!

Now you can use these features:

---

## 1. 💬 **AI Chat Tab** - Ask Questions About Your Wardrobe

Ask the AI fashion assistant questions like:
- "What should I wear to a wedding?"
- "What outfits can I make with my wardrobe?"
- "What colors go well together?"
- "How do I style my green t-shirt?"
- "What should I wear for a date?"

The AI will:
- ✅ Use your uploaded wardrobe items for context
- ✅ Give personalized fashion advice
- ✅ Suggest outfit combinations
- ✅ Provide styling tips

**Try it:** Go to the **Chat** tab and ask a question!

---

## 2. ✨ **Recommendations Tab** - Get Outfit Suggestions

Get AI-powered outfit recommendations:

1. **Select an Occasion:**
   - Date, Work, Party, Casual, Formal, etc.

2. **Optional: Select Body Shape:**
   - Rectangle, Apple, Pear, Hourglass, Inverted Triangle

3. **Click "Get Recommendations"**

The AI will:
- ✅ Analyze your wardrobe items
- ✅ Suggest complete outfits
- ✅ Explain why each outfit works
- ✅ Provide styling tips
- ✅ Consider your body shape (if provided)

**Try it:** Go to the **Recommendations** tab!

---

## 3. 👔 **Wardrobe Tab** - View Your Items

- View all uploaded items
- See AI-generated tags and details
- Check item stats (total items, total images)

**Note:** Since MongoDB isn't connected, items are shown in the upload response but not saved permanently. For persistence, connect MongoDB.

---

## 4. 🔍 **Analyze Image Tab** - Analyze Individual Images

Upload a single image to get detailed analysis:
- Item type, color, pattern, style
- Body shape compatibility
- Occasion suggestions
- Detailed description

**Try it:** Go to the **Analyze Image** tab!

---

## 5. 📊 **View Stats** - Check Your Wardrobe

In the Wardrobe tab, click **"View Stats"** to see:
- Total items in wardrobe
- Total images uploaded
- Summary of your collection

---

## 🎯 Recommended Workflow

1. **Upload Images** ✅ (You've done this!)
2. **Get Recommendations** - Try the Recommendations tab with different occasions
3. **Chat with AI** - Ask questions about styling and outfits
4. **Analyze More Images** - Upload more items to expand your wardrobe

---

**In Chat Tab:**
- "What should I wear to a casual party?"
- "Can I wear my green t-shirt with khaki pants?"
- "What accessories would go with my black pants?"
- "How do I dress for a job interview?"

**In Recommendations Tab:**
- Select "Date" occasion → Get date outfit suggestions
- Select "Work" occasion → Get professional outfit ideas
- Select "Party" occasion → Get party outfit recommendations
